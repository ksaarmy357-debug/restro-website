## 2026-08-02 - Accessible Accordions and Custom Cursor High-Contrast Focus Indicators
**Learning:** In design systems featuring custom cursors with `mix-blend-mode: difference`, standard interactive element focus outlines can easily become visually obscured or unreadable. Implementing high-contrast, themed focus indicators with `!important` color declarations and outer offsets (e.g., `outline-offset: 4px`) ensures focus states remain visible. Furthermore, when refactoring custom elements to semantic `<button>` patterns (such as WAI-ARIA accordions), heading-specific font and weight properties must be explicitly preserved on the interactive button itself to maintain the intended visual hierarchy.
**Action:** Always verify keyboard focus states visually using high-contrast outlines (like `var(--accent-amber) !important`) when custom mixed-blend cursors are present, and move heading aesthetics to the nested interactive tag during semantification of custom widgets.
