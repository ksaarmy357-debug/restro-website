## 2026-03-30 - Accordion WAI-ARIA and Keyboard Focus in Dark Luxe Design
**Learning:** In a highly stylized, futuristic dark-mode UI with custom cursors and luxury typography (e.g., Playfair Display), converting simple div click-handlers to native h3/button semantic wrappers preserves design aesthetics while opening the content to keyboard and screen-reader users. Using standard browser focus-visible outlines with a bright brand color (like amber) ensures high-contrast focus indicators remain visible even under mix-blend-mode differences.
**Action:** Always wrap interactive accordion headers in semantic heading buttons (h3 > button), toggle aria-expanded strings ("true"/"false"), hide non-text icons with aria-hidden="true", and style high-contrast :focus-visible outlines to match the brand identity.
