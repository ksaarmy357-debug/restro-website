## 2025-05-25 - [Accessibility: Semantic Buttons & ARIA States]
**Learning:** Converting icon-only 'div' toggles to semantic 'button' elements significantly improves keyboard accessibility (Tab navigation and Enter/Space support) and screen reader support via ARIA attributes.
**Action:** Always use semantic 'button' elements for interactive toggles and ensure 'aria-expanded' states are synchronized with visibility in JavaScript.
